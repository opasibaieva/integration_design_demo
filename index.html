Close CRM × ablefy Integration

Two-role prototype — Seller connects the CRM, Sales Team Member works the leads.
Seller
1
Start as Seller
You open as Sales Team Member. Click the role pill in the topbar to switch to Seller.
2
Connect Close CRM
Go to Opportunities → Connect CRM (or Settings → Integrations). Paste your API key and click Connect.
3
Map statuses (optional)
In Settings → Integrations → Close CRM, click "Map status between platforms" to align CRM stages with ablefy deal statuses.
4
Monitor in Opportunities
Seller sees all synced leads in read-only mode. Deals created by the team are highlighted.
Sales Team Member
1
Switch back to STM
Click the role pill again to return to Sales Member view.
2
Browse Opportunities
Leads from Close CRM appear in board or list view. Filter between New and Deal Created tabs.
3
Create a Deal
Click "Create a deal" on any lead. Review the pre-filled customer data from Close CRM, then complete the full deal form.
4
Track progress
Lead moves to "Deal Created" tab. Click "Open deal" anytime to revisit the deal form.
Prototype · not real data · click-through only
